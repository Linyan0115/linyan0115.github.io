## Research Experience

### Harvard AI and Robotics Lab

*Research Assistant · September 2026–present · Advisor: Prof. Mengyu Wang*

I study image geolocation privacy in vision–language models and help build a platform for collecting and curating decision-making data.

### Computational Optimization Research Program

*Research Assistant · March–November 2025 · Advisor: Dr. Yuqiang Ning*

I led a study on how turns and lane changes affect congestion and route choice. I developed a lane-level traffic assignment model and evaluated it using SUMO and the Sioux Falls benchmark network.

### Healthcare Operations Management Excellence Lab, University of Nottingham

*Research Assistant · November 2023–January 2025 · Advisor: Prof. Lei Liu*

I worked on computational optimization for operating-room scheduling and used reinforcement learning to study pricing decisions for perishable products.
